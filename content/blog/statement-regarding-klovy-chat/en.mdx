# Statement Regarding the Situation Surrounding the Klovy Chat Project

Dear everyone,

I have decided to write this statement because, for a long time, I have been carrying growing emotions about the situation surrounding the Klovy Chat project and the way I have been treated as its creator.

I am not writing this to start another discussion, attack anyone, or look for conflict. I simply want to share my perspective and explain why certain events affected me so deeply.

From the beginning, Klovy Chat has been a project created out of passion, ambition, and a desire to develop my own skills. It was not a project from a large company backed by a team of developers, administrators, and specialists. There was one person behind all of it — me.

From the start, I knew that creating a messaging app would not be easy. I knew there would be bugs, technical problems, and things that needed improvement. That is what building any larger project looks like, especially when you are learning many things on your own.

I have never claimed that Klovy Chat is a perfect project. I am aware that many things can be improved and developed further. Criticism itself is not a bad thing — on the contrary, it is necessary because it helps identify problems and look for better solutions.

The problem arises when criticism stops being about the project and starts being about the person.

Recently, many accusations have been directed at me which, in my view, were unfounded or stemmed from an incomplete understanding of the situation. Despite my attempts to explain certain matters, some people continued to hold on to their assumptions and treated me as if I were deliberately doing something wrong or had bad intentions.

I want to make this clear — I am not someone who wants to harm anyone.

I am a person who is learning, makes mistakes, and tries to fix them. I am not a large company with unlimited resources. I do not have a team of people responsible for every part of the project. I have my knowledge, my capabilities, and my limitations.

What hurts me most is that many people forget there is a real person behind the project.

Behind the name Klovy Chat, the code, and the servers, there is me — a person who also feels stress, pressure, sadness, and disappointment.

I would also like to mention the person who had a significant influence on the very beginning of this project's story — my former IT teacher at technical school.

It was during one of our more casual conversations about IT in June 2023 that the idea of creating my own messaging app was born. It was a very important moment for me, because that was the first time I began seriously thinking about creating something of my own, which later became Klovy Chat.

That is why it was even harder for me to accept that, over time, I no longer had her support.

I did not expect special treatment or for anyone to develop the project for me. I also did not expect anyone to promote it at all costs.

I did, however, hope for ordinary interest, a conversation, or some sign that the project — which also came about thanks to that inspiration — meant something.

I hoped Klovy Chat would at least be noticed in the school community, especially since it is a project related to IT and programming. I hoped it might interest other people who also wanted to develop their skills.

When that did not happen, I was simply sad.

I am not writing this to accuse my former teacher of having bad intentions. I do not know what her reasons were, and I do not want to make them up. I am only writing about how I perceived the situation and what I was feeling at the time.

Even so, I am grateful for the inspiration itself, because without those conversations, Klovy Chat probably would never have been created.

The whole situation surrounding the project also affected my personal life and mental health. The prolonged stress, constant pressure, need to explain myself, and feeling that I had to keep proving my intentions were very burdensome for me.

I am not asking for pity.

I am only asking for understanding.

Everyone has their limits, and everyone deserves basic respect, regardless of whether they are creating a small project or running a huge company.

It was because of my growing frustration and the feeling that my words and explanations were not being heard that I decided to stage a protest on October 30, 2026.

At 11:23 p.m. that day, the Klovy Chat API server was shut down.

This was not a decision made out of hatred or a desire to harm users. Above all, it was a way to show that things had gone too far and that the creator of a project also has limits.

I wanted to draw attention to the fact that behind the project there is not an anonymous company, but a person who has devoted their time, energy, and emotions to its development for many years.

I am aware that the protest was a difficult and controversial decision. But at that moment, it was a way for me to show that continuing to ignore the problem would not lead to any solution.

I would like the project itself to be what people judge first in the future — its capabilities, development, and direction — rather than the person trying to build it.

Klovy Chat may have bugs. It may need many improvements. It may not be a project for everyone.

But it was created out of genuine passion, a dream, and a desire to build something of my own.

I hope that, instead of more accusations, negative comments, and mutual misunderstanding, we can one day create space for a normal conversation, cooperation, and constructive criticism.

Thank you to everyone who continues to support me despite the difficulties, believes in this project, and appreciates the fact that someone is trying to build something of their own.

**Jakub Maksymowicz**  
Creator of Klovy Chat
